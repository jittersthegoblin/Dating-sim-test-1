# Heartglass — V1 Design Notes

## Core premise

The player accidentally touches the Heartglass in a human antique shop and is winnowed into a fae dimension. An unseen magical voice asks where their heart lies: toward a man or a woman. The answer selects Caelan or Caelia, two gender variants of the same kind, loving high-fae Warden.

14. Hidden fae marriage rite for the secret ending

## Relationship philosophy

Caelan/Caelia is a green-flag dateable: kind, respectful, protective without being controlling, capable of admitting mistakes, and responsive to boundaries. The healthiest choices tend to support the strongest outcomes, but the game does not reward blind agreement.

Several systems are intentionally hidden:

- Trust
- Bond
- Affection
- Respect
- Negative strikes
- Story flags

The player receives feedback through expressions, dialogue changes, later callbacks, available choices, and the Heartglass's vague state (Quiet / Warm / Glowing / Resonant) rather than visible numbers.

## Important remembered flags

Examples include:

- Whether the player studied the Heartglass carefully
- Whether the old marginal note was copied
- Whether a too-early kiss boundary was respected
- Whether the dateable's vulnerable confession at the lake was handled honestly
- Whether conflict about safety was handled as two equals
- Whether the player beat the dateable honestly at chess
- Whether the player was honest about missing home
- Whether the balcony became romantic/intimate
- Whether the player sought a way to keep both worlds rather than abandoning one

The hidden marriage ending requires a particularly healthy, attentive run plus the Heartglass lore clues. It is deliberately much harder than reaching romance or engagement.
